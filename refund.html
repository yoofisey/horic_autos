Refund Policy

Our policy on deposits, purchases, and hire purchase agreements.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Test Before You Buy

We strongly encourage every buyer to inspect the vehicle and take a test drive before paying. Vehicles are sold on an "as seen" basis, and we are transparent about each vehicle's condition, mileage, and service history. Because of this, all vehicle sales are final once payment is complete and the vehicle is collected.

2. Deposits and Reservations

Deposits used to reserve a vehicle are non-refundable if you decide not to proceed, but are applied in full to the purchase price when you complete the sale. If we are unable to deliver the vehicle for a reason within our control, your deposit will be refunded in full.

3. Refunds Before Collection

If you have made a full payment but the vehicle has not yet been collected, and you wish to cancel, we will refund your payment less any costs already incurred (such as registration, roadworthy certification, or customs processing) and subject to a reasonable administration fee.

4. Hire Purchase

Hire purchase agreements are governed by the written agreement you sign. In the event of default, the vehicle remains the property of the financing arrangement until all instalments are completed. Early settlement may be available with applicable discounts or fees as stated in your agreement.

5. Mechanical Warranty

Most vehicles come with a basic 3-month mechanical warranty. Warranty claims are handled by our team and do not constitute grounds for a refund. Extended warranty packages may be purchased at the time of sale.

6. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the laws of the Republic of Ghana. Where a vehicle is delivered with a serious defect that we cannot remedy, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund under applicable consumer protection law.

7. Contact

To request a refund or discuss this policy, contact us on WhatsApp at +233 53 262 7932, email info@accelerenmotors.com.
